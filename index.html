About

Most IT problems are not technical problems. They are coordination problems, trust problems, or documentation gaps that eventually show up in the infrastructure. My job is to find those gaps, fix them, and build something replicable so the next team does not face the same situation.

Over 10 years across K-12 education, higher education, healthcare, gaming, and big tech, that approach has produced results that scale past my direct involvement. At the University of Washington, I rebuilt a clinical department's broken relationship with IT, eliminated $30,000 a year in redundant spending, and developed an onboarding framework later used to centralize four additional departments. The JAMF Pro system I designed for the College of Arts and Sciences became UW's university-wide macOS MDM standard. At Meta, I identified that 5,000 engineers had been locked out of IT support due to a Linux compatibility gap nobody had addressed, ported the solution myself, and watched the documentation get incorporated as a first-party vendor product within two weeks. At Tencent, I built a gaming studio's complete IT infrastructure from zero in five months, and the identity stack I designed was adopted by two additional studios across North America.

I build with the assumption that what I make will eventually be used beyond its original scope.

Outside of work, I play harmonium, tabla, and dholki. I am building an AI-powered Sikh library trained on 758 million words of multilingual manuscripts and classical literature spanning English, Punjabi, Urdu, Hindi, and Devanagari script. That project reflects the same instinct that drives the infrastructure work: something valuable should not stay locked away where only one person can access it. I am grounded in Sikh philosophy and active in community and interfaith leadership. I speak English and Punjabi fluently, with conversational Hindi and Urdu. I bring the same level of loyalty and resilience to my professional deployments as I do to supporting Manchester United—remaining committed through every challenge.
